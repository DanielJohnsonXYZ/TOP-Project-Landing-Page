Privacy

How information is handled.

Last updated: 30 July 2026.

What information may be collected?

When you request a mini-scan, book a call or commission an audit, you may provide your name, work email, company, website, target market, priority products and commercial questions. Calendly and any agreed payment or document tools process information under their own privacy terms.

How is it used?

Information is used to respond to your request, assess fit, deliver the agreed work, communicate about the project and maintain basic business records. Public website and answer-engine evidence may be collected as part of the service.

Is website access required?

No account passwords, analytics access or website logins are required for the standard mini-scan or external audit. Optional internal data is only used when you choose to share it for the agreed work.

Is information sold or used to train models?

Your contact and project information is not sold. Information is not intentionally submitted for the purpose of training public AI models. Third-party tools may process data according to their own terms, so sensitive or regulated information should not be submitted without prior agreement.

How long is information kept?

Working files and correspondence are retained only as long as reasonably needed for delivery, follow-up, legal, accounting and operational purposes. You can request deletion where applicable, subject to records that must be retained.

Who operates the service?

Answer Engine Audit is a trading name operated by We Scale Startups. Privacy questions and access or deletion requests can be sent to daniel@wescalestartups.com.
