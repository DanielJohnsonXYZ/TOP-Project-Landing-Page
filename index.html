Who this is for

Brands where the buying decision depends on research, comparison or trust.

Strong fit

  • High-consideration ecommerce products
  • Technology, wellness, sports and specialist equipment
  • Brands competing in buyer guides and “best” queries
  • Ecommerce, growth, SEO or product marketing leaders
  • Teams that need to know why competitors are recommended

Not a strong fit

  • Businesses looking for guaranteed rankings or mentions
  • Very local services with little online research behaviour
  • Brands without an active product or usable website
  • Teams seeking a bulk automated report with no interpretation
  • Anyone expecting one prompt to represent all AI behaviour

Four failure modes

These are separate visibility problems, not stages in a funnel.

A brand may experience one, several or all four at once. The diagnostic identifies which failure mode is actually limiting discovery and recommendation.

01

Absent

Your brand does not appear when customers ask relevant buying questions.

Impact: no consideration at the point of research.
02

Misunderstood

AI systems associate you with the wrong category, use case or audience.

Impact: the right buyers receive the wrong reason to choose you.
03

Under-evidenced

Your strongest claims exist on your website but are not sufficiently corroborated elsewhere.

Impact: competitors look more credible even when your product is stronger.
04

Mentioned, but not chosen

You appear in the answer, but objections or weak positioning push the final recommendation elsewhere.

Impact: you create awareness while competitors capture demand.

Frequently asked questions

Method, reliability and what we need from you.

The diagnostic is designed to be transparent about uncertainty. AI answers change, so the report records conditions, compares patterns and avoids presenting one response as permanent truth.

How do you account for changing or inconsistent AI responses?

We do not treat one output as a definitive ranking. We use consistent prompts, record the date, market and platform, compare answers across systems, and look for repeated brands, caveats and cited sources. Stable source-level patterns receive more weight than one unusual model response.

Which AI platforms do you test?

The core diagnostic can test ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Google. Additional engines can be added when they are relevant and accessible, but the goal is not to inflate the platform count. It is to understand the buying journey that matters commercially.

Which countries, markets and languages can you test?

The standard scope supports English-language testing for the UK, US, Australia, Canada and relevant European markets. Other countries and languages can be scoped separately because prompts, sources and recommendations may change by language and location.

How are the buying questions selected?

We combine the product category, priority use cases, market terminology, competitor framing and common pre-purchase decisions. Client-supplied questions are useful, but we also test whether they reflect how customers actually compare and choose products.

Do you need analytics, website access or internal information?

No access is needed for the free mini-scan or core external diagnostic. We need the website, target market and priority product or category. Analytics, search data, customer research and internal positioning can improve the interpretation, but they are optional and only requested with permission.

What exactly do I receive?

The free mini-scan is a concise written finding. The full diagnostic includes a structured report covering prompt results, competitor visibility, cited evidence, SEO and GEO issues, the primary commercial gap, prioritised actions and a 45-minute readout.

How long does it take?

The free mini-scan is normally delivered within two working days. The full diagnostic is normally delivered within five working days after the scope and inputs are confirmed. Larger multilingual or multi-market scopes may require a separate timeline.

Do you guarantee that AI tools will recommend us?

No. AI systems change and no responsible provider can guarantee a mention or recommendation. The work identifies and improves the clarity, authority, corroboration and source signals that influence whether a brand is understood and selected.

How is information handled?

The mini-scan uses public information unless you explicitly share additional context. Any internal information is used only for the agreed work. We do not need account passwords for the standard diagnostic, and you are under no obligation to proceed after receiving the free finding.
