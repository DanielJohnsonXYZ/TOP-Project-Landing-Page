Visible, but not reliably recommended.

A gaming hardware brand had a strong editorial win and excellent product visibility. But other major sources repeatedly raised software and pricing objections, while competitors owned a clearer recommendation narrative.

The issue was not a lack of mentions. It was fragmented source consensus, which meant an AI answer could mention the brand positively and still recommend a competitor.

Questions

What the diagnostic is, and what it is not.

Is this just another SEO audit?

No. Traditional SEO is part of the analysis, but the diagnostic also examines third-party sources, entity clarity, corroboration, recommendation framing and the evidence AI systems use to form an answer.

Do you guarantee that AI tools will recommend us?

No. AI answers change and no responsible provider can guarantee a recommendation. The work improves the clarity, authority and evidence signals that influence whether a brand is understood and surfaced.

Who is this best for?

Brands selling products that require research, comparison or trust before purchase, especially technology, wellness, sports, specialist equipment and high-consideration ecommerce.
